Our story

A little of Paris, in Newington Green.

Belle Epoque's history is intimately tied up with Hulya and Eric's extensive travel experiences around the world.

2000

It all began when Eric met Hulya, over a conversation about their long lasting love for Africa.

2002

An 18th century listed building in the heart of Stoke Newington became a pastry shop. 37 Newington Green opened its doors in November and very quickly became a popular destination for Francophiles as well as locals.

2012

A new kitchen and centralised production in Highbury Barn, a stone's throw from N16.

2014

A boutique on Upper Street in Islington, since moved to a bigger site at 267 Upper Street.
