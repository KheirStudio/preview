Bonnington Square

In the heart of historic Bonnington Square.

One of the longest surviving experiments in urban living in Europe.

Many of the young idealists who squatted the neighbourhood in the late 1970s and 1980s still live here, and are often to be found hanging around, and shooting the breeze...
