Our range

Four ranges. Tap one to open it.

FoamUpholstery, packaging, acoustic and more

Furniture and upholstery foam

Cut to size or shape. Ideal for furniture in the home, garden, boat or caravan.

Packaging foam

Used in film and theatre for props and costumes, and for packing delicate electronic equipment.

Reticulated foam

Open-cell foam, mainly in beige or black, for air and pond filtering. Its high tear strength suits puppet and costume making. Sheets 200cm x 75cm and 200cm x 62cm, from 6mm thick.

Polyethylene foam (Plastazote)

Flexible closed-cell foam for props, costumes and packaging. FR and non-FR, in 2m x 1m sheets.

Acoustic egg-box foam

For sound proofing and packaging.

Chip foam

Reconstituted polyurethane in various densities, for packaging, upholstery and costume making.

Foam strip and cord

For upholstery and costume making.

Latex sheeting

A high-quality natural material for seating and mattresses, cut to your own requirements.

RubberSheeting, matting, tiles, cord and tubing

Rubber sheeting

Expanded neoprene, EPDM and nitrile PVC, solid natural and synthetic rubbers, reinforced, food-grade, anti-static and silicone sponge. Plain or self-adhesive backed.

Rubber matting

Fine, flat and broad fluted in black, grey and white. Widths 914mm, 1000mm and 1200mm; 3mm to 12mm thick. Electrical matting to BS921.

Rubber tiles and rolls

A variety of colours. Tiles 50cm square, rolls 10m x 1m.

Rubber and neoprene cord and strip

Solid and sponge cord in a variety of sizes, plain or self-adhesive backed.

Self-adhesive rubber strip

Neoprene or EPDM strip in a variety of widths and thicknesses.

Rubber and PVC tubing

Plastic tubing and hosing in all sizes.
