Services

A service you can rely on.

Whether you are sending your goods to a family member or a business associate, our priority is a reliable, first class service.

Door to door services

We take your shipment from the door at the originating address, transport it to the destination country, clear it through customs and deliver it direct to the door at the destination address.

  • Door to doorCollected anywhere in the UK, delivered to your specified address in Nigeria.
  • Door to officeCollected from your door in the UK, for collection at our office in Nigeria.
  • Office to doorBring the goods to our office in London, delivered direct to the door.
  • Office to officeBring the goods to our office in London, for collection at our office in Nigeria.

Air freight

From the United Kingdom to Nigeria and a host of EU and African countries, consolidated or direct. We use premium airfreight carriers with established routing for a competitive and reliable service.

Sea freight

Containers, break bulk, flat racks, LCL and gateway consolidations. We handle all sea shipments originating from the United Kingdom to Nigeria seaports.

Storage services

If you need to store your items prior to shipment, our storage facilities are secure. Contact us for storage prices, which are based on the number of boxes and how long the goods stay with us.
