The MND design process

From a first meeting to the finished room.

Our designers provide expert advice and can guide the whole process from start to finish.

  1. Book an initial meeting at our showroom to discuss your individual requirements.
  2. Review the products available and develop the client brief with our design team.
  3. A dedicated member of our design team is appointed to look after your project.
  4. Your designer can visit your home to measure and survey the space if required. There is a charge for this service.
  5. Your designer presents floor plans and sketch proposals at the studio.
  6. Your designer fully discusses the products proposed for your home.
  7. Your designer discusses the design detailing and the other options available.
  8. When the design is finalised, your designer orders the products, manufactured by our worldwide partnership suppliers and shipped to the UK.
  9. Your designer personally assists with overseeing the installation process.
  10. Your designer remains available for design input on further projects.
