Our story

Three generations of Rahims.

Longing for the food we left behind, our journey to find routes to import these products started, and before long we opened our doors to the public in the 80s. Our first store was launched in East London, at the heart of the Bangladeshi community.

We became the UK's first Bangladeshi owned cash and carry, supplying caterers around the country, and now across Europe. Today a third generation is amongst us: a generation of new ideas.
