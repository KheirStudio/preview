Customer advice

Caring for marcasite jewellery.

  • Never use silver dip. It causes irreversible damage to the silver and marcasite stones, giving a brown, burnt appearance.
  • Keep it away from water. Always remove marcasite jewellery before hand washing, cleaning, bathing, showering or swimming. Most marcasites are set with water-based epoxy, and the stones may become loose.
  • Avoid chemicals. Detergents, oils, perfumes, make-up and hairspray can cause tarnishing and damage.
  • Clean gently. Wipe jewellery clean with a soft cloth when needed.
